About

SHIFT NYC is a techno promoter in New York City. Long-format raves, raw sound and serious curation: industrial, modular, breaks and Detroit techno. We work across Brooklyn, Queens and Manhattan, alone and with partners like CRUD0, Nova Noir and Circuit Structure Records. Tickets are sold only on Resident Advisor.
